Shock Wave Therapy: A Breakthrough in Pain Management

May 25, 2024 Pain Management 7 min read
ADVANCED PAIN MANAGEMENT

Understanding Shock Wave Therapy

SWT involves the use of high-energy acoustic waves to target areas of pain or injury. These waves penetrate deep into the affected tissues, stimulating cellular repair mechanisms and promoting healing. Unlike invasive procedures, SWT is safe, efficient, and typically requires minimal downtime.

01
KEY ADVANTAGES

Benefits of Shock Wave Therapy

Shock Wave Therapy may be considered for different musculoskeletal conditions. Its potential benefits include the following:

Pain Relief

  • SWT may help reduce pain associated with conditions such as plantar fasciitis, tendonitis, and heel spurs.
  • It may help improve comfort and mobility, supporting a return to everyday activities.

Support for Tissue Healing

  • Acoustic waves may stimulate biological responses involved in tissue repair.
  • Treatment may influence local blood flow and processes associated with collagen formation and tissue recovery.

Non-Invasive Treatment

  • SWT is performed externally without surgical incisions.
  • It generally does not require anesthesia and is delivered as an outpatient treatment.

Versatility

  • It may be used for selected tendon and soft-tissue conditions, sports injuries, and certain chronic musculoskeletal pain conditions.
  • Treatment is selected according to the condition and clinical assessment.

Functional Improvement

  • Some patients experience improvements in pain and function after a course of treatment.
  • Results vary depending on the condition, severity, and individual response.
02
TREATMENT APPLICATIONS

Is Shock Wave Therapy Right for You?

Shock Wave Therapy may be considered for individuals experiencing persistent musculoskeletal pain or selected sports-related injuries that have not improved sufficiently with initial conservative treatment.

It is commonly considered for certain tendon and soft-tissue conditions, depending on the diagnosis and suitability of treatment.

Plantar Fasciitis

A condition involving irritation and pain around the plantar fascia, commonly causing discomfort around the heel or bottom of the foot.

Tendonitis

May be considered for selected tendon conditions, including Achilles tendinopathy and tennis elbow.

Calcific Shoulder Tendinopathy

A shoulder condition involving calcium deposits within a tendon, which may cause pain and restricted shoulder movement.

Hip and Knee Pain

Certain conditions, such as iliotibial band-related pain and patellar tendinopathy, may be assessed for appropriate treatment.

Chronic Back Pain

People experiencing persistent back pain require an appropriate diagnosis before considering SWT. It is not a treatment for every spinal or disc condition.

Consulting a qualified healthcare provider, such as the team at Mobility Masters, can help determine whether Shock Wave Therapy is an appropriate option for your specific condition.

03
THE TREATMENT PROCESS

How Does Shock Wave Therapy Work?

Shock Wave Therapy uses a specialized device to deliver acoustic waves to a targeted treatment area. The physiotherapist identifies the affected region and selects appropriate treatment settings according to the patient's condition.

  • The affected area is assessed before treatment.
  • A treatment applicator is positioned over the selected area, usually with a coupling gel.
  • Acoustic waves are delivered through the applicator using suitable treatment settings.
  • The patient's response is monitored throughout the session.
  • Further rehabilitation advice and exercises may be recommended when appropriate.

The number and frequency of sessions depend on the diagnosis, treatment response, and clinical recommendations.
