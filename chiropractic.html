ABOUT CHIROPRACTIC CARE

Support Spinal Health. Move With Confidence.

Chiropractic care is a hands-on healthcare approach that focuses on the assessment and management of musculoskeletal conditions, particularly those involving the spine and joints.

Depending on your condition and clinical assessment, treatment may include spinal manipulation, joint mobilisation, soft tissue techniques and movement guidance.

CHIROPRACTIC METHODS

Treatment Approaches Focused On Movement

Treatment techniques are selected according to your clinical assessment, condition and individual suitability.

01

Spinal Manipulation

Specific manual techniques that may be used to address selected spinal movement concerns following appropriate screening.

02

Joint Mobilisation

Controlled joint movements used to assess and support mobility where clinically appropriate.

03

Soft Tissue Therapy

Manual techniques directed at muscles and surrounding tissues to address tension and movement restrictions.

04

Corrective Exercises

Guided exercises to develop strength, flexibility and better movement control.
