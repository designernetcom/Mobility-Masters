UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What Is Laser Therapy?

Laser Therapy, also known as photobiomodulation, uses specific wavelengths of light to interact with body tissues. Depending on the equipment and treatment settings, it may help support cellular activity and the body's natural recovery processes.

It is used in physiotherapy as a supportive treatment for certain painful musculoskeletal conditions, including selected joint, muscle and tendon problems.

At Mobility Masters, our physiotherapist assesses your symptoms and medical history before recommending Laser Therapy as part of an individualised treatment plan.
