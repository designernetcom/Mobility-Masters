UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What Is TECAR Therapy?

TECAR stands for Transfer of Energy Capacitive and Resistive. It is a form of radiofrequency therapy used in physiotherapy to deliver energy to body tissues through specialised equipment.

Depending on the treatment mode and settings, TECAR Therapy can be used to create controlled thermal or non-thermal effects in selected tissues. It is commonly incorporated into rehabilitation programmes for certain muscle, joint and soft tissue conditions.

At Mobility Masters, our physiotherapist evaluates your symptoms and physical condition before recommending TECAR Therapy as part of your individual treatment plan.

TREATMENT METHODS

How TECAR Therapy Works

TECAR treatment uses different energy delivery modes selected according to the affected tissue and rehabilitation requirements.

01

Capacitive Mode

Uses a capacitive electrode and is commonly selected for treatment of more superficial, water-rich tissues such as muscles and soft tissues.

02

Resistive Mode

Uses a resistive electrode and may be selected to target tissues with higher resistance, depending on the clinical objective.

03

Controlled Energy Delivery

Energy intensity and treatment duration are adjusted according to the equipment, treatment area and patient comfort.

04

Rehabilitation Exercises

Suitable strengthening, stretching and mobility exercises may be included to support functional recovery.
