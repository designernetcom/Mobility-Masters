01
COMMON CAUSES

Why Neck & Back Pain Are Increasing in IT Professionals

Modern work routines often involve prolonged sitting and continuous screen exposure. Without proper ergonomics and regular movement, these habits can place additional stress on the neck, shoulders, and spine.

  • Continuous sitting for 8–10+ hours
  • Incorrect laptop height and office chairs
  • Forward-head posture
  • Weak core and tight upper back muscles
  • Sedentary lifestyle

Over time, these factors can contribute to muscle imbalance, nerve irritation or compression, headaches, stiffness, and increased stress on spinal discs.
