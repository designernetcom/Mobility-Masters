UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What Is Shock Wave Therapy?

Shock Wave Therapy uses controlled acoustic pressure waves directed towards a specific area of the body. These waves may stimulate local tissue responses and support the healing process in certain musculoskeletal conditions.

It is commonly considered for selected long-lasting tendon and soft tissue problems, especially when symptoms have not improved sufficiently with basic treatment approaches.

At Mobility Masters, treatment suitability is decided after understanding your symptoms, medical history and movement requirements.
