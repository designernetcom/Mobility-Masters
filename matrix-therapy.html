UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What Is Matrix Therapy?

Matrix Therapy is a term used for specialised treatment approaches that work with the body's tissues and physiological responses. The exact method depends on the technology and equipment used by the clinic.

It may be included in a physiotherapy programme to complement other rehabilitation methods for selected muscle, joint and soft tissue conditions.

At Mobility Masters, treatment recommendations are based on your symptoms, physical assessment and functional goals. The specific Matrix Therapy technology and its suitability should be explained before treatment begins.
