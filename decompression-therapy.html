UNDERSTANDING THE TREATMENT

What Is Decompression Therapy?

Decompression Therapy is a form of spinal traction that uses a specialised table or equipment to apply controlled pulling forces to the spine. The aim is to create gentle changes in spinal loading and reduce pressure on selected structures.

Depending on the condition, it may be considered for certain cases of back or neck discomfort, disc-related symptoms and nerve irritation.

At Mobility Masters, our physiotherapist first evaluates your symptoms, medical history and movement before deciding whether decompression is an appropriate treatment option.
